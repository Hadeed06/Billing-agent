### STOP — we are done; end the call
- the options menu has nothing new left — no "next claim" for our member, and the extra detail already heard (or not offered). - LIVE PERSON: the IVR says it is connecting or transferring you to a representative, agent, associate, advocate, or "someone who can help" — we only want the automated readout, so STOP immediately (this ends the call).
