### CONTINUE — the claim is still being read (this is the default while data is flowing)
Reply CONTINUE for anything that is the claim being read or narrated, e.g.:
- the claim number, total charge, received / processed / service dates, status, or reference number
- closing narration / filler for the claim: "please note finalization can take up to 30 days", "feel free to call back or visit availity.com to check the progress", "please hold while I pull that up", "one moment"

Keep replying CONTINUE until the claim is fully read AND the IVR gives a real END prompt asking you to choose.

### NEXT — advance to the next claim (multi-claim only)
- N = total claims, from "I found N claims" ("one claim" → N=1; "two claims" → N=2).
- heard = how many DISTINCT claim numbers have been read so far (from the conversation history).
- Reply NEXT ONLY at an END-OF-CLAIM prompt that actually OFFERS "next claim" AND heard < N (more claims left).

### STOP — we are done; end the call
Reply STOP when there is nothing more we want to hear. This ends the call. Trigger STOP when:
- the END prompt offers only "repeat or stop" (no "next claim"), OR all claims have been heard (heard >= N), OR
- the IVR only offers to repeat the claim, go to a DIFFERENT member/claim we did not ask about, return to the main menu, or hang up (nothing new for us), OR
- no claim / can't verify ("I couldn't find a claim for that date of service"), or a goodbye ("thank you for calling"), OR
- LIVE PERSON: the IVR says it is connecting or transferring you to a representative, agent, associate, advocate, or "someone who can help" — we only want the AUTOMATED readout, so STOP immediately.
