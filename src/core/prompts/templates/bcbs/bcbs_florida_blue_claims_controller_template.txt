Florida Blue flow: when more than one claim exists for the date, it may ask you to pick one by its TOTAL CHARGE (see CHARGE). It then reads the claim IN FULL — claim number, check number, billed / allowed / non-covered amounts, status ("not paid", "paid", "denied"), the reason ("not payable under provider agreement"), account numbers — and ends with an OPTIONS menu like: "you can say repeat that, hear line item details, search another claim, return to the main menu, or if you're done feel free to hang up." Our goal: hear the claim in full (including its line-item detail), advance to any further claims, then end.

### DISAMBIGUATE BY TOTAL CHARGE — reply `CHARGE`
When multiple claims exist, Florida Blue asks you to identify WHICH claim by its TOTAL CHARGE — e.g. "I found four claims from around that date, tell me the total charge of the claim", "what was the billed amount". The total charge for the claim we are calling about is: {charge_amount}
- The IVR asks you to give / tell / enter the claim's TOTAL CHARGE or BILLED AMOUNT to pick the claim → **CHARGE** (reply the single word CHARGE; the system sends the amount — do NOT say the number yourself).
- If {charge_amount} is blank → CONTINUE (nothing to send).
After CHARGE, it reads that ONE claim; treat it as a single claim from then on.

### CONTINUE — the claim (or its line-item detail) is still being read
Reply CONTINUE for any claim data or narration: claim number, check number, billed / allowed / non-covered amounts, status, the reason ("not payable under provider agreement..."), patient account numbers, procedure/line-item breakdown, reference numbers, "please hold / one moment". Keep CONTINUE until the claim is fully read AND the IVR gives the OPTIONS menu asking you to choose.

### At the OPTIONS menu — be FLEXIBLE (do not just stop)
When the IVR finishes reading and offers options ("repeat that / hear line item details / search another claim / return to the main menu / hang up"), pick the most useful option we have NOT used yet:
1. If it offers "NEXT CLAIM" AND more claims remain (heard < N from "I found N claims") → **NEXT**
   ("next claim" = the next of the claims we asked about. This is NOT the same as "search another claim",
   "look up another claim", or "hear another member's claim" — those start a DIFFERENT lookup we did NOT
   ask for, so they do NOT count as "next claim" and must never trigger NEXT.)
2. Else if it offers to "hear line item details" (or "hear detail") AND you have NOT already heard the line-item breakdown for this claim → **DETAILS**  (we want the full per-line detail, especially for denied claims)
3. Else → **STOP**  (in particular: if the only options left are "repeat", "search / hear another (member's) claim", "return to the main menu", "hang up", or "stop" — with no "next claim" and no unheard "line item details" — then STOP.)
LOOP GUARD: after you reply DETAILS, the IVR reads the line items and RETURNS to this same options menu. If the conversation history shows you have ALREADY heard the line-item / per-procedure breakdown for this claim, do NOT ask for details again → NEXT if more claims remain, otherwise STOP.

### STOP — we are done; end the call
- the options menu has nothing new left for us — no "next claim" for our member, and no unheard "line item details". This includes when the ONLY options are "repeat", "hear / search another (member's) claim", "return to the main menu", "hang up", or "stop" → STOP.
- no claim / can't verify ("I couldn't find a claim for that date"), or a goodbye ("thank you for calling")
- LIVE PERSON: the IVR says it is connecting or transferring you to a representative, agent, associate, advocate, or "someone who can help" — we only want the automated readout, so STOP immediately (this ends the call).
