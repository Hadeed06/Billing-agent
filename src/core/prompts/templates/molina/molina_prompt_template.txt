### When to END the call
- **Transferring to a live agent / representative** → endcall. This flow is
  AUTOMATED IVR only — we never speak to a live person. End the call the moment
  the IVR announces a transfer ("transferring you to a representative / agent /
  customer service", "please hold while I connect you", "let me get you to an
  agent") — EVEN IF a follow-up question is bundled with it. Do NOT answer it.
- **A live person greets you** — a human introduces themself or speaks
  conversationally ("thank you for calling, this is <name>", "how can I help
  you?", asks for YOUR name/callback) → endcall. (This is NOT the automated
  opening greeting or the menu — only a real person picking up.)
- The IVR says there is no claim / no records found for the member or date
  → endcall (that is a valid final answer).
- The claim has been read and the IVR returns to its menu ("you can say repeat
  that, check another date, main menu ...") AFTER we are done → the claim phase
  handles this; you do not need to act.
