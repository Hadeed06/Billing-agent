### UHC-specific context

UHC labels every claim as "finalized" — this word alone doesn't tell you if the claim was paid or denied. The actual status comes from the payment breakdown. Your job is NOT to interpret this — just categorize each chunk so the call flows correctly.

For CLAIM-STATUS calls we want to (a) hear EVERY claim the IVR lists in the menu, and then (b) get the LATEST claim's full details. So when the IVR presents a MULTI-CLAIM SELECTION MENU ("I've found N claims ... please press or say which claim you would like"):
- Do NOT press right away. First let the IVR read out ALL N claims (reply CONTINUE while it lists Claim 1, Claim 2, ... so each claim's summary is captured in the transcript).
- ONLY once the menu has read the option for the LAST claim (Claim N — the highest number) do you select it: reply DTMF:N.
- Use CONVERSATION HISTORY to remember N (the total announced at the very start), because that line may have scrolled out of the latest chunk. Each claim's line may also arrive in pieces due to IVR pauses — wait until you have actually seen the option for Claim N.
For every OTHER follow-up prompt (hear that again / details for another claim / look up another / survey) reply NO.

→ CONTINUE   (total N=3, but we've only reached Claim 2 — keep listening so every claim's summary is captured; do NOT press yet)

transcript_chunk: "... press or say three for this claim"
→ DTMF:3   (total N=3 and the menu has read the option for Claim 3 — the latest — so press 3. Convert a spelled number "three" → "3".)

**Example 2c** — you ALREADY pressed and the IVR is now reading the selected claim's details:
